Methodology. SPI = weighted combination of normalized indicators (0–100), following the OECD/JRC 10-step composite-indicator framework (normalize → align direction → weight → aggregate → test robustness).

  • Normalization & direction alignment: Nardo, M., Saisana, M., Saltelli, A., & Tarantola, S. (2008). Handbook on Constructing Composite Indicators: Methodology and User Guide. OECD/EC-JRC.
  • Weight derivation: Saaty, T.L. Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP), pairwise comparisons, Consistency Ratio ≤ 0.10 threshold.
  • Combining several indicators into one rankable score, and treating community-reported needs as a weighted input: DSWD Listahanan Proxy Means Test; CBMS Composite Index / Multidimensional Poverty Index.
